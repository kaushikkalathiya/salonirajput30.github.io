Comestey

WooCommerce store built with Gutenberg blocks for dynamic product layouts and custom PHP styling.

Company
Triospire Web Solution Pvt. Ltd.
Role
Senior WordPress Developer (CMS)
Period
May 2023 to Jul 2024
Category
WooCommerce & Gutenberg

Problem

Comestey needed a WooCommerce store with dynamic product layouts that the client team could update easily, without the page weight of a third-party page builder.

Approach

Built the storefront using WordPress Gutenberg blocks and reusable block patterns, paired with custom PHP templates and CSS styling for WooCommerce product displays, taxonomies and attributes.

Technologies

WooCommerceGutenbergPHPCSS

Outcome

Delivered a lightweight, block-based WooCommerce catalogue that allows non-technical editors to manage products and categories directly.

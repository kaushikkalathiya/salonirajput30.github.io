SmartSolar

Converted a static HTML website into WordPress using ACF and custom post types for product and content management.

Company
Triospire Web Solution Pvt. Ltd.
Role
Senior WordPress Developer (CMS)
Period
May 2023 to Jul 2024
Category
HTML to WordPress · ACF & CPT

Problem

SmartSolar's static HTML website required developer edits for every product or specification update, preventing the team from maintaining their catalogue independently.

Approach

Migrated the static HTML site to WordPress, registering custom post types and Advanced Custom Fields (ACF) field groups for structured product specifications, imagery and responsive templates.

Technologies

WordPressACFCustom Post TypesPHPResponsive Design

Outcome

Replaced static HTML product pages with a structured ACF and custom post type architecture that the client team can update directly from the WordPress admin.

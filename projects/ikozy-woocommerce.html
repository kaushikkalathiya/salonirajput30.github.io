Ikozy WooCommerce

Full ecommerce store with custom WooCommerce template overrides, dynamic product grids with filtering and sorting, and a customised one-page checkout.

Company
Narola Infotech Pvt. Ltd.
Role
Software Engineer
Period
Jan 2025 to Nov 2025
Category
WooCommerce Ecommerce

Problem

Ikozy needed a full WooCommerce store whose design, product discovery and checkout requirements went beyond what default WooCommerce templates provide, while keeping Core Web Vitals acceptable on product and checkout pages.

Approach

Custom template overrides

Built custom PHP template overrides for product listing, single product, cart and checkout pages alongside Elementor Pro layouts.

Product grids and one-page checkout

Implemented dynamic product grids with filtering and sorting, and customised a one-page checkout flow using WooCommerce hooks and filters.

Asset loading

Optimised WooCommerce script loading and product image loading to keep storefront performance steady across devices.

Technologies

WooCommerceElementor ProPHPJavaScriptCSS

Outcome

Delivered a production WooCommerce storefront matching the supplied design references with a streamlined one-page checkout and controlled script and image loading.
